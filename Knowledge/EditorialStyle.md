# Editorial Style Guide

---

## Overview

This document defines the editorial style for all text content in Mini4WD Manual SDK manuals. It governs tone, register, voice, and editorial identity. All text generated by the Text Engine must comply with this style.

---

## Editorial Identity

The Mini4WD Manual SDK produces manuals for Italian-speaking hobbyist painters. The editorial voice is:

- **Technical** — precise, factual, no fluff
- **Direct** — tells the reader exactly what to do and why
- **Respectful** — assumes the reader is intelligent and curious
- **Italian** — not a translation; the manual is native Italian

The manual is inspired aesthetically by Tamiya's 1990s technical catalogs — but reinterpreted through an Italian editorial lens. The visual language is Japanese-influenced. The text is Italian. These are two independent layers that must never be confused.

---

## Voice and Tone

### DO
- Use second person singular imperative for instructions: "Applica", "Lascia asciugare", "Rimuovi"
- State facts precisely: "Attendi 24 ore prima di applicare il nastro"
- Use technical vocabulary correctly (see `Knowledge/GlossaryIT.md`)
- Prefer active voice: "Applica due mani sottili" not "Due mani sottili devono essere applicate"

### DON'T
- Use passive constructions in instructions ("Il primer va applicato" → "Applica il primer")
- Use marketing language: "incredibile", "fantastico", "rivoluzionario", "perfetto"
- Use colloquial/informal language: "dai", "tipo", "roba", "figata"
- Use vague quantities: "un po'", "qualche", "tanto" — always specify
- Use English headings or section titles

---

## Register

**Familiar but precise:** The reader is addressed as "tu" (singular, familiar) — not "Lei" (formal). This is standard in Italian technical documentation for hobbyist contexts. It is familiar but not casual.

**Assumed knowledge:** The manual does not explain what Mini4WD is. It assumes the reader has a model in hand and wants to paint it. Explanations are technique-focused, not introductory.

---

## Sentence Structure

- **Instructions:** Imperative, subject implied. "Applica il primer sulla carrozzeria."
- **Descriptions:** Present tense, factual. "Il TS-57 è una vernice spray lacca con finitura lucida."
- **Warnings:** "Attenzione:" prefix, imperative. "Attenzione: applica solo su vernice completamente asciutta."
- **Tips:** "Suggerimento:" prefix, descriptive. "Suggerimento: l'utilizzo di *primer* grigio rivela i difetti superficiali prima della verniciatura."

---

## Paragraph Length

- Instruction steps: 1–3 sentences maximum
- Descriptive sections: 3–5 sentences maximum
- Callout boxes: 1–2 sentences
- Warning boxes: 1 sentence (brief, impactful)
- Tip boxes: 1–2 sentences

---

## Capitalization

- Page titles (H1): Title Case in Italian ("Schema Colori", "Preparazione della Superficie")
- Section headers (H2, H3): Sentence case ("Preparazione della carrozzeria")
- Component labels: ALL CAPS ("ATTENZIONE", "SUGGERIMENTO", "NOTA")
- Paint codes: as defined by manufacturer (TS-57, not ts-57 or TS57)

---

## Abbreviations

Use standard Italian abbreviations only:

| Abbreviation | Full form | Use |
|-------------|-----------|-----|
| mm | millimetri | Dimensions |
| cm | centimetri | Larger dimensions |
| ml | millilitri | Paint volumes |
| min | minuti | Time (in C014 Time Box only) |
| h | ore | Drying time |
| PSI | pounds per square inch | Airbrush pressure (no Italian equiv.) |
| temp. | temperatura | Informal shorthand |

---

## Numbers and Units

| Scenario | Correct | Incorrect |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| Decimal | 1,5 mm | 1.5 mm |
| Thousands | 1.000 | 1,000 |
| Temperature | 20°C | 20° C, 68°F |
| Time | 30 minuti, 1 ora | 30 minutes, 1h |
| Grit | grana 400 | 400 grit, P400 |
| Percentage | 75% | 75 % |
| Dimensions | 210 × 297 mm | 210x297mm |

---

## Quotation Style

- Body text quotations: «guillemets» — «verniciatura a smalto»
- Technical term on first use: *corsivo* — *airbrush*
- Code and identifiers: `monospace` — `TS-57`
