# Decal Application

**Document ID:** KNW-DEC-001
**SDK Version:** 2.2.0
**Category:** Technique Reference

---

## Overview

Decals (water-slide transfers) are the final decorative element before clear coat. Proper application determines whether the finished model looks professional or amateur.

---

## Types of Decals

| Type | Application | Durability |
|------|-------------|------------|
| Water-slide | Soak in water, slide onto surface | Low without clear coat |
| Dry transfer | Burnish directly onto surface | Medium |
| Self-adhesive sticker | Peel and stick | Low |

Mini4WD kits typically include water-slide decals and self-adhesive stickers. Water-slide decals produce the most professional result.

---

## Water-Slide Decal Process

### Materials Required
- Decal softener (Tamiya Mark Fit, Micro Sol)
- Decal setter (Micro Set)
- Small bowl of room-temperature water
- Tweezers
- Soft flat brush (size 2–4)
- Cotton swabs

### Process

1. **Apply gloss coat to receiving surface.** Decals do not conform to matte or textured surfaces. Surface must be gloss.
2. **Cut decal close to artwork.** Leave 0.5mm of clear film around design.
3. **Soak in water 15–30 seconds.** Decal should slide freely; if not, soak longer.
4. **Slide decal from backing onto surface.** Do not lift decal by itself — slide it directly.
5. **Position carefully.** Use wet brush to move decal into position.
6. **Remove excess water.** Blot (do not wipe) from center outward with cotton swab.
7. **Apply decal softener.** Brush softener over decal — it will wrinkle momentarily (normal).
8. **Allow to dry completely.** Minimum 2h; 12h before clear coating.
9. **Do not touch softened decal.** The softener makes it very fragile until cured.

---

## Common Decal Defects

| Defect | Cause | Prevention |
|--------|-------|------------|
| Silvering | Air trapped under decal | Apply to gloss surface; use softener |
| Tearing | Over-softened or handled too soon | Wait for full cure before touching |
| Lifting edge | Decal dried before conforming | Apply more softener; use setter |
| Milky appearance | Excess moisture | Blot more thoroughly |

---

## Clear Coat Over Decals

After decals are fully cured (24h):

- Apply clear coat to seal and protect decals
- Never apply clear coat to fresh decals (causes wrinkling/dissolving)
- Use semi-gloss or gloss clear for best decal integration
- Matte clear coat over gloss decals creates visible edge lines
