## Application Methods

### Spray Can (Rattle Can)

**Best for:** Base coats, large uniform areas, quick production
**Tamiya products:** TS series, PS series

**Technique:**
1. Shake can vigorously for 2 minutes minimum (must hear the mixing ball)
2. Test spray on scrap paper — verify atomization is fine (not spitting)
3. Hold can 25–30cm from surface
4. Use smooth, sweeping passes — never stop moving while spraying
5. Apply 2–3 thin coats; allow 15–20 min flash time between coats
6. Full cure: 24h at room temperature

**Common errors:**
- Spraying too close: orange peel texture, runs
- Spraying too far: dust/dry spray, grainy texture
- Cold can: poor atomization — warm in hand before use

### Brush Painting

**Best for:** Detail areas, small components, touch-ups
**Tamiya products:** X series (gloss), XF series (flat)

**Brush types:**

| Brush | Use Case |
|-------|----------|
| Flat 0/2 | Large flat areas |
| Round 0 | General detail |
| Round 000 | Fine detail, panel lines |
| Liner | Straight lines |

**Technique:**
1. Thin paint to cream consistency (1:1 paint:thinner for first coat)
2. Apply in one direction only per coat
3. Second coat perpendicular to first (cross-hatching)
4. Minimum 3 coats for opaque coverage
5. Sand lightly with 1000 grit between coats if brush strokes visible

### Airbrush

**Best for:** Smooth gradients, precision masking, professional finish
**Recommended:** 0.3–0.5mm nozzle for Mini4WD scale

**Pressure:** 15–25 PSI for lacquers; 12–18 PSI for acrylics
**Thinning:** 1:1 to 1:2 (paint:thinner)
**Distance:** 10–15cm

---

## Paint Sequence Principles

1. **Light to dark:** Apply lighter colors first to avoid contamination
2. **Large to small:** Base coat entire body; then mask and add accent colors
3. **Base before metallic:** Metallic/pearl coats need a base coat for adhesion
4. **Flat before detail:** Apply base colors before hand-painting details

## Drying Times

| Paint Type | Flash Time | Recoat | Full Cure |
|-----------|------------|--------|-----------|
| Tamiya TS Lacquer (spray) | 15 min | 30 min | 24h |
| Tamiya X Acrylic (brush) | 20 min | 1h | 24–48h |
| Mr. Color Lacquer | 10–15 min | 30 min | 24h |
| Vallejo Acrylic | 15–20 min | 45 min | 24h |

*Times at room temperature (20°C). Humidity above 70% extends drying significantly.*
