# Clear Coat and Finishing

**Document ID:** KNW-CLR-001
**SDK Version:** 2.4.0
**Category:** Technique Reference

---

## Overview

Clear coat is the final protective layer applied over completed paintwork and decals. It unifies the surface finish, protects decals, and achieves the desired final sheen.

---

## Clear Coat Types

| Finish | Sheen | Best For |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Gloss clear | High shine | Racing liveries, candy finishes, decal protection |
| Semi-gloss | Medium shine | Realistic vehicle finishes |
| Matte/Flat clear | No shine | Military, weathered, or natural looks |
| Satin | Between gloss and matte | Leather-like or natural finishes |
| UV-cut clear | Variable | Prevents yellowing on white/yellow bodies |

---

## Application

**Spray can (recommended for Mini4WD):**
- Tamiya TS-13 (Gloss), TS-80 (Matte)
- Mr. Super Clear UV Cut (Gloss or Flat)

**Application technique:**
1. Ensure all decals are cured (24h minimum)
2. Apply thin even coats from 25–30cm
3. 2–3 coats for decal protection; 1 coat for basic protection
4. Allow 30 min between coats
5. Full cure: 24–48h

---

## Clear Coat Interactions

| Base Paint | Clear Coat | Result |
|-----------|------------|--------|
| Lacquer | Lacquer | ✅ Safe |
| Acrylic | Lacquer | ⚠️ Risk of dissolving — test first |
| Acrylic | Acrylic | ✅ Safe |
| Lacquer | Acrylic | ✅ Safe |

**Rule:** Lacquer clear coat on acrylic base = risk. Always test on scrap first.

---

## Premium Finishing Techniques

### Polishing (for gloss finish)
After gloss clear coat has cured 48h:
1. Wet sand with 2000 grit
2. Wet sand with 3000 grit
3. Polish with Tamiya Polishing Compound (Fine)
4. Polish with Tamiya Polishing Compound (Finish)
5. Optional: car wax for final protection

### Candy Finish
1. Apply silver metallic base coat
2. Mask as needed
3. Apply transparent color coat (e.g., Tamiya X-24 Clear Yellow over silver = gold candy)
4. Apply gloss clear coat
