# Glossary

**Document ID:** KNW-GLS-001
**SDK Version:** 2.2.0
**Category:** Reference

---

A reference list of technical terms used throughout the Mini4WD Manual SDK and in generated manuals.

---

## A

**Acrylic paint:** Water-based paint. Easy to clean, lower odor than lacquers. Slower drying than lacquer. Compatible with most surfaces.

**Airbrush:** Precision spray tool using compressed air to atomize paint. Provides finest control over coverage and gradients.

## B

**Base coat:** The first color layer applied to a primed surface. Forms the foundation for subsequent layers.

**Body (carrozzeria):** The painted polycarbonate or ABS shell of the Mini4WD model.

## C

**Candy finish:** A translucent color coat applied over a metallic base, creating a deep, jewel-like appearance.

**Clear coat (vernice trasparente):** Transparent protective topcoat applied over completed paintwork.

**Coat:** A single layer of paint applied in one session.

## D

**Decal:** A pre-printed design transferred to the model surface. Most commonly water-slide type.

**Degreasing:** Removing oils and contaminants from a surface before painting.

## F

**Flash time:** The minimum time between coats to allow solvent evaporation.

**Finish:** The surface appearance of a paint: gloss, matte, satin, metallic, pearl.

**Fish eye:** A painting defect consisting of circular craters caused by surface contamination.

## G

**Gate:** The connection point between a model part and the plastic sprue. Must be cleanly removed.

**Gloss:** A shiny, reflective paint finish.

## L

**Lacquer:** Fast-drying, solvent-based paint. High gloss, excellent durability. Strong solvent — incompatible with acrylic undercoats.

**Lifting:** Paint peeling when masking tape is removed, caused by inadequate cure time.

## M

**Masking:** The process of protecting painted areas with tape or fluid before applying a new color.

**Matte / Flat:** A non-reflective paint finish.

**Metallic:** A paint containing metallic flake particles, producing a sparkling reflective appearance.

## O

**Orange peel:** A surface defect resembling orange skin texture, caused by incorrect spray technique.

## P

**Pearl:** A paint containing pearlescent particles, producing a subtle color-shift and shine.

**Polycarbonate (PC/Lexan):** The transparent plastic used for Mini4WD body shells. Requires PS-series paints from Tamiya.

**Primer (primer/fondo):** A preparatory coating applied before the base color to improve adhesion and reveal defects.

## R

**Rattle can:** Common term for aerosol spray paint cans.

**Run / Drip:** A painting defect where excess paint flows downward before drying.

## S

**Satin:** A paint finish midway between gloss and matte.

**Silvering:** A decal defect where trapped air creates a silver halo around the decal.

**Sprue:** The plastic framework holding model parts in the kit. Parts are cut free and gates sanded smooth.

**Surface primer:** See Primer.

## T

**Top coat:** See Clear coat.

**Thinner:** A solvent used to reduce the viscosity of paint for brush application or airbrush use.

## U

**UV-cut clear:** A clear coat containing UV absorbers to prevent yellowing and color fading over time.

## W

**Wet sand:** Sanding technique using water as lubricant to reduce dust and achieve finer results.
