# Surface Preparation

**Document ID:** KNW-PRE-001
**SDK Version:** 2.4.0
**Category:** Technique Reference

---

## Overview

Proper surface preparation is the single most important factor in achieving a professional paint finish on Mini4WD polycarbonate bodies. Skipping or rushing preparation steps causes adhesion failures, fish-eye effects, and uneven finishes that cannot be corrected after painting.

---

## Preparation Sequence

### Step 1: Removal from Sprue

- Cut body parts from sprue with sharp sprue cutters — never twist
- Leave 0.5mm of gate, then trim flush with a hobby knife
- Sand gate marks with 400 grit, then 600 grit

### Step 2: Washing

Remove mold release agents and manufacturing oils:

1. Wash in warm water with mild dish soap
2. Use a soft brush (toothbrush works) for recessed areas
3. Rinse thoroughly — no soap residue
4. Air dry or use compressed air — never use cloth (leaves fibers)

**Do not skip this step.** Mold release agents cause paint to bead and peel.

### Step 3: Sanding (if required)

| Grit | Purpose | Technique |
|------|---------|-----------|
| 400 | Remove seam lines, gates | Circular then straight |
| 600 | Remove 400 scratches | Light pressure, wet sand |
| 800 | Final smoothing | Wet sand |
| 1000+ | Ultra-smooth finish | Wet sand, optional |

Wet sanding: use water with a drop of dish soap as lubricant.

### Step 4: Surface Degreasing

After sanding, degrease before priming:

- Wipe with isopropyl alcohol (IPA) 70% or higher on a lint-free cloth
- Do not touch the surface with bare hands after degreasing
- Allow to dry completely (2–5 min) before priming

### Step 5: Priming

Primer improves adhesion and reveals surface defects:

- **Tamiya Surface Primer (spray):** Fine gray primer, reveals imperfections
- **Mr. Surfacer 500/1000:** Filler primer for deeper scratches
- Apply 2 thin coats, 10 min between coats
- Allow 1h minimum before painting; 24h ideal

### Step 6: Final Inspection

After primer has cured:

- Examine under angled light for low spots, scratches, fish-eye
- Sand imperfections with 600–800 grit wet
- Re-prime affected areas
- Repeat until surface is completely uniform

---

## Mini4WD-Specific Notes

- **Polycarbonate bodies:** PS series spray paints are formulated for polycarbonate. Using standard TS lacquers on polycarbonate may cause crazing. Verify body material before selecting paint.
- **ABS bodies:** Standard TS lacquers work well. Follow normal prep sequence.
- **Lexan/Clear bodies:** Paint from the inside for protection. Reverse color order.
