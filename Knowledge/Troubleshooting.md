# Troubleshooting

**Document ID:** KNW-TRB-001
**SDK Version:** 2.2.0
**Category:** Reference

---

## Surface Defects

### Orange Peel
**Description:** Dimpled texture resembling orange skin
**Cause:** Paint applied too thick, can held too close, paint too cold
**Remedy:** Let cure fully (24h). Wet sand 1000→1500→2000. Apply thinner final coats.

### Fish Eye
**Description:** Circular craters in paint surface
**Cause:** Oil or silicone contamination on surface (fingerprints, mold release, silicone lubricant)
**Remedy:** Strip and restart. Degrease thoroughly with IPA. Ensure no silicone products nearby when painting.

### Runs / Drips
**Description:** Paint flows downward before drying
**Cause:** Too much paint applied, too close, or too slow passes
**Remedy:** Let cure fully. Sand smooth with 800 grit. Re-apply thinner coats.

### Dry Spray / Grainy Texture
**Description:** Rough, sandy surface texture
**Cause:** Can held too far, paint dried before reaching surface, low humidity
**Remedy:** Polish with fine compound. For severe cases: wet sand 800→1000→1500.

### Crazing / Cracking
**Description:** Network of cracks in paint surface
**Cause:** Incompatible paints (usually lacquer solvent attacking acrylic base)
**Remedy:** Cannot be repaired — must be stripped. Always use compatible paint systems.

### Paint Lifting During Masking Tape Removal
**Cause:** Applied tape to uncured paint, or using aggressive tape on fragile surfaces
**Remedy:** Touch up with brush. For large areas: repaint and wait full cure before next masking.

## Decal Defects

### Silvering
**Cause:** Air trapped under decal on non-gloss surface
**Remedy:** Apply decal softener, press down firmly, allow to re-dry.

### Decal Dissolving
**Cause:** Applied lacquer clear coat over fresh/wet decals
**Remedy:** Cannot be reversed. Strip, repaint, re-apply decals, cure fully before clear coat.

---

## Prevention Checklist

Before each painting session:
- [ ] Work area temperature 18–24°C
- [ ] Humidity 40–60% (check with hygrometer)
- [ ] No silicone products within 2 meters
- [ ] Hands washed; handle model with gloves after degreasing
- [ ] Paint shaken/stirred thoroughly
- [ ] Test spray on scrap before applying to model
